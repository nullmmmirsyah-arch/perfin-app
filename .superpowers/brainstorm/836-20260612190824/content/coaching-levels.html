Seberapa "Coach" Dashboard Ini?

Pilih level yang menjadi target vision untuk fase ini

A

Insight — Decision Support

"You're on track. Spend up to Rp137k today."

Praktis, langsung actionable. Sesuai PRD.

B

Coach Ringan

"You're on track. Last week you spent 8% less! You can spend up to Rp120k today."

Insight + konteks historis + positive reinforcement

C

Coach Penuh

"Morning! You're doing great. Food needs attention (15% ahead), but Transport is on track. Safe spend: Rp120k today."

Personalisasi, edukasi, tone ramah, multi-konteks

D

Coach Adaptif

Seperti C + belajar dari kebiasaan: "I notice you tend to overspend on weekends. Want to set a weekend limit?"

Behaviour-aware, butuh ML/heuristics

💡 Bisa pilih kombinasi: misal A untuk MVP, C untuk long-term vision.
